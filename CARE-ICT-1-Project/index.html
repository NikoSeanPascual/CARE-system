Terms & Conditions & Privacy Notice

1. Purpose of the System

The C.A.R.E. (Campus Health, Medical Assessment, and Record Entry System) is designed solely for routine campus clinic visit logging, symptom self-assessment, triage queue generation, and health summary passes[cite: 1].

2. Data Privacy & Local Storage

In compliance with client-side requirements, your submitted personal details (Student ID, Name, Grade, Section, Emergency Contact) and consultation history are processed and stored locally within your browser's local storage[cite: 1]. No sensitive data is transmitted to external third-party servers[cite: 1].

3. Medical Disclaimer

This platform is intended for basic health assessment and clinic logging on campus[cite: 1]. It does not provide automated emergency medical response or replace professional medical triage during life-threatening severe emergencies[cite: 1].

4. User Responsibilities

Students must provide accurate emergency contact details and truthful health information to assist clinic nurses and staff in delivering proper on-campus care[cite: 1].
